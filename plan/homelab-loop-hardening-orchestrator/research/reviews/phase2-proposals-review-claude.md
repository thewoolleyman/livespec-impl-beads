## Executive verdict

All six are well-researched — roughly thirty implementation and journal claims re-verified, the overwhelming majority holding exactly as stated, including every measurement in the §§04/05 re-measurement. But three carry findings that block ratification as written: `acceptance-rework-state-machine`'s capacity clause directly contradicts the ratified `count(active) < wip_cap` condition while claiming self-containment; `loop-probe`'s mandatory terminal-`done` assertion is unreachable under the ratified default `acceptance_mode`; and three of the six declare new settings "NOT API-configurable" against a ratified clause reading "Every setting MUST be settable via the orchestrator API", which none of them cites or amends. Two further defects survive review precisely because they look settled: `needs-attention-verdict` ratifies a three-verdict vocabulary while a live fourth verdict (`NO_CHANGE_NEEDED`) ships in the very module it cites, and both rework-touching proposals rely on a journal record that ratified text says twice does not exist (it does — the ratified text is stale, and neither proposal fixes it). Scenario numbering across all eleven pending proposals is clean.

2. **Blocker** — loop-probe's terminal-`done` assertion is unreachable under the ratified default `acceptance_mode: ai-then-human` (a PASSING pass parks in `acceptance` awaiting the human `accept`).
